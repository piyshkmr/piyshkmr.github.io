Common questions

FAQ

I know hiring a performance marketer is a big decision. Here are answers to the questions I get asked most often.

How long before I start seeing results?
Every business is different, but most start noticing improvements within the first 2–4 weeks after launching campaigns. The first few days are focused on gathering data and testing what works. Once we identify winning audiences and creatives, results start to scale quickly and consistently.
What makes you different from other marketers or agencies?
I don't treat marketing like experiments or guesswork. Every decision I make is backed by data and a clear goal to grow your business profitably. I stay transparent at every step, sharing what's working, what's not, and what we'll do next. My aim is simple: make your ads pay off and your growth predictable.
Can you fix my existing campaigns?
Definitely. If you already have ads running but they're not performing well, I can audit your account to find what's holding them back wrong targeting, weak creatives, or tracking issues. Then I'll rebuild or adjust your campaigns to improve efficiency and boost returns.
Can you help if I've never run ads before?
Absolutely. I'll handle everything from setting up your ad accounts and installing tracking to launching your first campaigns. Even if you're starting from zero, I'll make sure you feel confident about your ads.
What's included in your ad management service?
It includes everything you need to run profitable campaigns: audience research, creative strategy, campaign setup, ongoing optimization, and weekly reporting. I handle everything, so you can focus on running your business while I focus on growing it.
